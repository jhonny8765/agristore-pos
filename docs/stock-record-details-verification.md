# Stock record details and simultaneous owner actions

October 27, 2026. Quantities and costs in these checks are TEST examples. They do not establish the cause of the historic reported shortage or certify physical inventory or prolonged use on an actual cashier PC.

## Reproduced gap and fix

Held/pending stock identity and measured entry depended on the downloaded product catalog. If another employee created a product after a window downloaded that catalog, a fresh stock-control response could show that product without its name, lot or measurement. The owner could not choose ml for the held outcome. The loss-cost form also required the product in the local catalog, even though the authorized report already identified the loss.

Authorized stock-control responses now carry the current product name, base measurement, product-matched lot and expiry. The client retains its explicit legacy fallback and never borrows another product's lot or invents an unknown measurement. An outcome without any known measurement is disabled with refresh guidance. Loss rows now carry only their own configured purchase-unit IDs, names and exact factors; the owner can document cost without that product in the local catalog. No purchase/retail price is supplied as assumed loss cost. Missing evidence stays unvalued. New issues and checkout still require their usual downloaded catalog and authorization.

The migration decorates read responses only after the existing command chain's current session, active membership, organization/branch, role and snapshot checks. Pagination counts, cursors, order, quantities, valuation totals, cost-history versions, stock movements and cash writes are unchanged. No new table, dependency, table grant or write action is introduced.
