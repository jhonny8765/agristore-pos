## Next software work, in order of stock-control value

Blind counts and independent approval are implemented, fully software-verified and assigned to staging. Rehearse the [three-person procedure](blind-counts.md) with actual staff before the pilot. The connected [expiry and investigation procedure](expiry-and-investigations.md) is also delivered: owner/manager assignments, deadlines, overdue/unassigned/mine filters, retained progress evidence and owner-only resolution. Assignments and notes keep cases open and stock unchanged; pagination retains old unresolved cases. The [purchase-cost report procedure](purchase-cost-loss-reports.md) is delivered: documented per-unit costs, visible unvalued rows, retained corrections, separate authorised-use values and complete-range quantities by branch/product/lot. It does not verify the historical reported amount or its cause. The [purchasing procedure](purchase-orders-and-deliveries.md) is also delivered: owner-approved requests, partial/mixed/full rejection inspections, held-goods linkage, explicit remaining order quantities, supplier claims and retained credit/return references. These records do not send orders or settle supplier accounts. Remaining software priorities follow.

The [receiving correction procedure](delivery-reversals.md) is delivered for untouched inspections: owner-only preview, complete linked reversal, original-ID recovery and one linked corrected inspection. Later stock/count/hold/claim activity and partly closed orders block automatic reversal. Older quick receipts, opening imports and dependent-history corrections still require owner reconciliation. Never enter the same physical delivery twice to fix the first record.

1. **Expanded receiving corrections, when specified.** Define a separate owner reconciliation process for older quick receipts and receipts with dependent activity; retain every dependent sale, hold, claim and deliberate order closure. The untouched-inspection reversal must not be broadened into a force button.
The [partial-refund procedure](partial-refunds.md) is delivered for selected original quantities: cumulative centavo allocation, explicit financial versus physical quantities, separate saleable/held goods, remaining refundable quantities, owner/shift/drawer checks and original-ID recovery. Partial refunds block the old full-refund path. The [mistaken-refund correction procedure](refund-corrections.md) is delivered for the latest untouched refund: full recovered cash, owner preview, linked opposite stock movements, untouched held-return cancellation, immutable originals, original-ID recovery and one linked corrected replacement. Incomplete legacy evidence and dependent activity require owner reconciliation. Arbitrary price-only concessions and exchanges remain separate workflows requiring explicit design; do not simulate them with false quantities.
2. **Broader operational reports and maintenance.** Add approved report requirements, storage/queue health review, clearer reauthentication feedback after a long-idle window, and repeatable maintenance/backup ownership. Preserve history rather than introducing automatic deletion to reduce size.

Until these are implemented, use the documented [daily routine](stock-control-operations.md) and explicit manual records for the gaps. Do not describe them as delivered features. Keep customer credit, electronic payment processing, full accounting and official tax-invoice issuance outside the initial pilot's agreed scope.

[Measured-unit selling](measured-units.md) now covers rope meters, a one-liter/ml entry switch and whole pieces, alongside existing kg/gram/peso entry. Receiving, refunds and stock reports retain the same configured base conversion. Verify the real roll/container definitions and measuring devices before the pilot.
