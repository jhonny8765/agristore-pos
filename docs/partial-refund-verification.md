Follow the [owner procedure](partial-refunds.md). This is a cash refund against selected original quantities, not arbitrary price-only credit, a product exchange, automatic supplier/order correction, tax-invoice issuance or full accounting. A new refund requires an online owner with the owner's own open shift in the original branch, adequate recorded drawer cash, inspected quantities and a current refund version. The server preserves immutable original receipts, validates against cumulative original quantities and serializes writes with the branch stock commands.

Money handover and physical truth remain staff responsibilities. Save once and hand over the confirmed amount once, retaining signed evidence. An interrupted save must be recovered before paying again. Goods arriving after their financial refund require owner physical reconciliation, not a second cash refund. Real staff/drawer/scale rehearsal, cashier-PC endurance/restarts, actual cloud-backup restoration and a reconciled pilot remain open. These software checks do not certify prolonged hardware operation or guarantee loss prevention.
