October 27, 2026. Software checks use disposable TEST goods and simulated costs. They do not establish a real shortage's cause, verify the reported historic loss, measure actual goods or certify prolonged use on an unchosen cashier PC.

## Delivered controls

The selected product/lot and measured draft stay together; changing the selection clears old quantity and unit mode. A successful issue resets both. Submission alone changes no stock; owner approval remains required. Whole pieces stay whole and quantities beyond the six-decimal stock precision are refused rather than rounded.

Held-goods release, disposal and supplier-return entry uses the same exact helper, with the remaining held quantity as its bound. Liquid held/issue rows and loss rows show canonical liters plus an exact ml equivalent. Release alone adds saleable stock; disposal and supplier return do not deduct saleable stock a second time. Original role, expiry, branch, quantity-lock and permanent-ID protections are unchanged.

The owner purchase-cost form previews the removal value from the entered documentary cost of one selected unit. Changing that unit clears the cost amount to prevent accidentally carrying a container price over as a liter price. The preview uses high-precision decimal arithmetic and rounds once per complete row; PostgreSQL remains authoritative on save. Missing evidence stays unvalued, cost corrections preserve earlier versions, and supplier returns/authorised use remain separate from the loss subtotal. CSV and reconciliation keep the stated canonical base quantities. No schema, grant, dependency or privileged key was changed.
