# Local customer-credit verification

November 3, 2026. This records a local implementation and evidence, not a published release or official invoice-system approval. No hosted schema migration, real customer creation, live sale, collection, refund or stock mutation was performed by this task.

## Delivered locally

Owner-approved customer profiles/limits/terms; online credit sales with an optional exact cash down payment; mandatory unique actual manual-booklet invoice reference; server-calculated agreed Net 30/60/90 dates; physical stock posting once; partial per-invoice collections; audited collection reversals; quantity-based credit returns splitting debt reduction and real cash refund; receivable totals/aging; current credit records, per-event acknowledgements, loaded-row CSVs and complete loaded statements.

The original cash sale payload/version remains unchanged. New credit sales use an online command and version-2 payload with an explicit payment mode. Original stock, cash and financial records remain immutable. Uncertain credit operations use the existing durable request/recovery mechanism. Holding/resuming retains credit selection and original attempted request. Cash checkout waits for an uncertain credit request and, after confirmation, a causally newer stock snapshot. A download started before credit confirmation cannot clear that refresh fence. Previously saved cash requests can still be retried.

Credit clients/invoices/events are RPC-only. Branch/session/role checks are enforced server-side. Owner credit policies use versions. Client, invoice, drawer and stock locks protect transaction balances; the same request is serialized and compared to its retained payload. Shared request/audit journals have additional restrictive policies so stock-only staff cannot read copied client billing details through those tables.
