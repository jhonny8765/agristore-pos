## Implemented boundary

An owner can undo the latest active mistaken full or partial refund when its financial and physical evidence is complete and untouched. The original receipt, refund, original drawer and evidence remain immutable. A correction records the full original recovered/retained cash as a positive payment in the correcting owner's open shift, reverses only the original saleable stock additions through linked opposite movements, and cancels untouched held returns through retained internal disposition events. It does not classify those cancellations as a stock loss or release them into saleable stock.

The preview checks original payments and physical effects, current ledger revision, monotonic financial version, later active refunds, later same-product/lot stock movements, approved physical counts, processed holds, return-investigation activity and other open branch shifts. Confirmation checks again under the branch lock and row locks. The owner must attest to all cashier PCs being synchronized and baskets cleared, actual goods and the full cash amount. The server cannot see an offline PC's unsent records or physically count cash.

A permanent correction ID and exact stored request identify an attempt. Identical retries acknowledge the original result even after the drawer closes; changed contents conflict. Current authenticated sessions, active membership and owner/branch authorization still apply. A new selected-quantity replacement can link to the correction; one direct replacement is allowed. Full correction of an old legacy refund without recorded physical evidence is blocked. There is no force override or partial recovered-cash undo.

Rope stock now supports canonical meters. Product setup includes cut-meter selling and explicit roll conversions with a separate retail-meter price when enabled. Liquids remain canonical liters and a one-liter selling unit exposes a liter/ml entry switch. Whole pieces remain enforced. Switching displayed liquid units changes the entered representation, not the saved quantity or price. Sealed bottles may remain pieces. The app does not convert weight to volume.
